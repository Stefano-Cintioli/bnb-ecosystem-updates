# Adapted working prompts

These are reusable versions of the working brief, with local paths and internal review details removed. They are not a verbatim export of private chat history. Grok helped with the original research and prompt; the presenter supplied the creative feedback.

## Research brief

Prepare an English monthly BNB Chain ecosystem update. Use stories with a public @BNBCHAIN post. Treat any presenter-approved additional article card as a separate sourced metric. Record the exact URL, displayed date, number, qualification and supporting artwork for each claim. Do not invent facts or infer that an announcement is currently live. Separate confirmed claims from questions requiring a human check.

Quote numbers exactly. Use “eligible USDC, USD1 & U.” If bStocks backing is mentioned and supported, use only “1:1-backed by a real share.” Avoid APR, yield, price, passive income and guaranteed claims in authored viewer copy. Use no em dashes, partner tags or hashtags.

## Production brief

Work in the existing folder. Use HyperFrames 0.8.114 and FFmpeg, with Node 22 or later. Use the original provided logo paths, a sober dark background and consistent typography. Build and approve the intro first, then the outro, then the script/claims, then the cut.

Keep the presenter centered in horizontal 1920×1080 video at 30 fps. Show the original announcement images large and proportional. Keep captions in one ALL CAPS font, with white text and one current yellow word. Smooth recording starts and stops while preserving complete speech. Keep footage, renders and local transcriptions out of Git.

Only the Builder writes and renders; reviewers read only. Preserve each accepted output and use isolated folders for revisions. Show real encoded previews and key frames. Never infer stakeholder approval.

## Later feedback examples

- Show the BNB Chain logo first, then “Ecosystem Updates.” Add a subtle effect to the symbol only.
- Use a short, smooth TV switch-off outro with the same music fading down.
- Keep all caption text in the same capitalization and font. Show announcement images consistently large. Keep community links in the reply.
- Accelerate the presenter portion to 2×, keeping the intro/outro at normal speed and preserving voice pitch. Recheck caption pace and joins.
- Try a mild background blur on a short moving sample before applying it throughout.

## Monthly handoff

Reuse the accepted intro/outro and start at Gate 3. Populate the script, shot list and sign-off templates with the new month’s exact claims and dates. The new-episode generator remains planned; these prompts do not implement it.
